Homework — homework/lesson-4/:

  Build a fresh themed scratch page (your content, your theme) that reproduces today's whole arc independently, as one cohesive
  feature rather than three separate drills:

  - A <nav> with real navigation links, and separately, at least one <img> wrapped in an <a> linking out to an external site (with
  target="_blank" and rel="noopener noreferrer") — sitting outside the <nav>, since it's content, not wayfinding.
  - A <form> with a text input that adds a new item to a visible list on submit — no page reload, input clears after submitting,
  and the element reference is grabbed once (via a const holding the element, not the extracted value) and reused for both reading
  and clearing.

  That last part is really the crux of today — if you can build that cleanly from scratch without hitting the const-reassignment
  error again, it's a good sign it's actually internalized rather than just pattern-matched.
