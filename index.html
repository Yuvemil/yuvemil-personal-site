Who is Void?

Void, that is to say me, is a name I chose for myself as an alias for my real, legal name. The name follows naturally with my interest of the void, eldritch, space, and being alone. Maybe it is because I am a solitary individual, one who stays away from others, and one who enjoys listening to the babble of their mind, that I chose this name. In any case, thank you for spending your time scanning the drizzle of thoughts in front of your eyes; it means... something to me, though I'm not sure what.

Can Void eleaborate on their interests?

Yes, I can. In fact, here they are:

  1. Space, eldritch, and the beauty that exists in the stars.
    • I enjoy the feeling of having the stars surround me; being in the middle of their cold embrace.
    • Although I actually have not read many Eldritch stories, I love the idea: creepy, cruel, and space-related monsters. Maybe I love the idea itself instead of the monsters: that there are bigger things out there than our warm planet.
  2. Video games, from time to time.
    • I do enjoy my fair share of video games from time to time. I've recently been playing TF2 and League of Legends again--games that I haven't played in quite a while. It's fun.
