Bad Ending

You've done it; you made the cure. Now, the virus is gone forever thanks to your amazing potion. Millions, and even future billions are saved and you are hailed a hero everywhere you go.

As you celebrate in your million dollar home, surrounded by smiles, loud music, and a sticky floor at your feet, you get a text message. Your heart would've dropped if it weren't for the alcohol keeping it afloat.

The next month, your neigbours report a stinking smell from your apartment.

After the cops knock on your door and receive no answer, they barge in for their welfare check, only to then be freighted by the sight of your decomposing corpse next to a knocked over wooden chair.

You are buried with a great title on your tomb: "Great Chemist Who Saved The Future of Humanity". Next to it, was your pet rat's grave: "Here lies Dumbo the Rat. Cause of Death: A Cure for Humanity's Problems."

...Is what you saw after using your time machine! Now, with your new found knowledge, you know exactly what different decisions to do the... next time... the last time..?

Use time machine to get back to the start?
